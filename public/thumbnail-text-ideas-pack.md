# Thumbnail Text Ideas Pack
### 60 short, high-impact phrases proven to work on YouTube thumbnails

## How to use this pack
Thumbnail text works best when it's 2-5 words, in a large bold font, and creates curiosity without fully explaining the video. Pick a phrase below that matches your video's angle, or combine two short phrases (one at the top of the thumbnail, one at the bottom).

## Design tips for pairing text with thumbnails
- Keep contrast high: light text on a dark background or dark text on a light background — never both mid-tone
- Use a bold, thick font (thin fonts disappear at small sizes on mobile)
- One phrase, not a full sentence — the thumbnail should tease, the title should explain
- Leave visual breathing room — don't let text overlap the main subject's face
