Help / FAQ

Why didn't the transcript show up for my video?
Some videos don't have YouTube captions (auto-generated or manual) yet. If none exist, there is nothing to extract — this usually resolves itself within a day or two of upload as YouTube processes auto-captions.

Why are there no tags or hashtags?
Not every video has public tags or hashtags in its description. The tool only shows what the uploader actually included.

I hit a daily limit — what now?
The free tool allows a limited number of extractions per day. The limit resets automatically the next day.
