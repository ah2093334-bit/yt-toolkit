    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is this YouTube transcript extractor free?

    Yes, completely free, with a daily limit of 5 extractions per visitor to keep the tool available for everyone.

    Why doesn't every video have a transcript?

    The transcript comes from YouTube's own captions (auto-generated or manually uploaded). If a video has no captions yet, there's nothing to extract — this is a YouTube limitation, not a tool limitation.

    Do I need to sign up or install anything?

    No. Paste the link, click Extract, and the transcript appears — no account, no browser extension, no software.

    Can I copy the transcript in one click?

    Yes, every result includes a "Copy" button that grabs the entire transcript as one block of text.

    Does this tool download the video?

    No. It only reads publicly available caption text and metadata — no video or audio file is ever downloaded, keeping the tool aligned with YouTube's terms of service.

    Also available: full metadata extractor (title, tags, thumbnails), subtitle downloader, and video-to-text converter.
