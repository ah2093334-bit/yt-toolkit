What we collect

AllVidExtract does not require sign-up and does not collect personal information from visitors. The video links you paste are sent to our server only to fetch public metadata (title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions) and are not stored.

Video metadata is retrieved using the official YouTube Data API. Please also refer to Google's Privacy Policy for how YouTube/Google handles data.
