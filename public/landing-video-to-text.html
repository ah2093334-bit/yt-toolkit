    Frequently Asked Questions

    What does "video to text" actually convert?

    It pulls the video's existing text data — title, description, and the transcript from YouTube's captions — rather than generating new text from the video's audio track.

    Can I use this to write a blog post from a video?

    Yes — many creators use the extracted transcript as the base for a blog post, restructuring it with their own headings afterward.

    Is this free?

    Yes, free with a limit of 5 extractions per day per visitor.

    What if the video has no captions?

    Title and description will still be extracted, but the transcript section will note that no captions are available for that specific video.
