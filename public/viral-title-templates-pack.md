# Viral YouTube Title Templates Pack
### 50 fill-in-the-blank templates used across top-performing YouTube videos

## How to use this pack
Each template has a `[blank]` where you insert your own topic, number, or claim. Swap the bracketed word for your subject and the title is ready to use. Templates are grouped by the psychological trigger they use — curiosity, urgency, social proof, or transformation — so you can match the style to your video's actual content.

## Tips for using these templates well
- Keep the final title under 60 characters so it doesn't get cut off in search results
- Only use "viral" language (nobody talks about, secret, truth) if your video actually delivers on that promise — mismatched titles hurt retention and channel trust
- Test 2-3 title variations on the same video idea before publishing if your platform allows it
