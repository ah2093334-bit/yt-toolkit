About AllVidExtract

AllVidExtract is a free tool built for creators, editors, and marketers who need quick access to a video's metadata — title, description, tags, hashtags, thumbnail, and transcript — without opening ten different tabs.

What it does today

Paste any YouTube link and get everything back in seconds: title, full description, tags, hashtags, downloadable thumbnails in multiple resolutions, the transcript (when captions are available), and a set of related topic ideas to help plan your next video.

What's coming

Support for Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, and Telegram video metadata is on the roadmap. The goal is one place for every platform's video data.

What it will never do

AllVidExtract only reads public metadata and captions. It does not download video or audio files, keeping it aligned with every platform's terms of service.
